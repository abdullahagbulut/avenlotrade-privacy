AvenloTrade Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

AvenloTrade uses eBay account information only to provide services authorized by the user, including listing management, inventory management, order processing, and related seller functions.

Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted through eBay, AvenloTrade may access account, listing, inventory, order, fulfillment, and related seller data.

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the AvenloTrade application and perform actions explicitly authorized by the eBay account holder.

Data Sharing

AvenloTrade does not sell eBay user data. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality or meet legal and security requirements.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke AvenloTrade's access to their eBay account at any time through their eBay account settings.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the AvenloTrade account owner through the contact information associated with the service.
